Facilities

Everything under
one roof

Three spaces, one membership: the strength floor, a dedicated cardio centre, and a recovery suite most gyms treat as a paid add-on.

01 — Strength

Equipped
properly

The cable system is the heart of this floor — one of the most complete in Bahrain, with the stations, heights and attachments to train an angle properly rather than the one the machine was built for.

Dumbbells in three sets of every weight, so the pair you want is on the rack when you want it. Plate-loaded and selectorised machines for chest, back, shoulders, arms and legs — with the adjustment range to fit you properly, not “close enough.” Plates and weight trees at every machine, so nobody is carrying discs across the floor.

Squat racks, fourteen benches and growing, and the room to actually use them.
