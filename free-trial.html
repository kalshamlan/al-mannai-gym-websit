Questions

Before you come

Is the trial really free?

Yes. Your first visit is free, once per person, for first-time visitors. No card and no commitment.

What should I bring?

Your CPR or passport. Wear sports clothing and closed athletic shoes on the training floor.

When can I come?

Any time. We are open 24/7, every day. Choose your day and hour in the form, and your confirmation comes on WhatsApp.

Can I use the sauna and jacuzzi on my trial?

Yes. Train on the floor, then use the recovery suite: the sauna, the steam room and the hot and cold jacuzzi.

Who can join?

Men aged 15 and over.

Is there parking?

Yes, on site at Al Alawi Mall, Nuwaidrat.

What happens after the trial?

Nothing, unless you want to join. If you do, reception signs you up on any of the plans above. There is no joining fee.

Is there a trainer to help me?

Yes. A trainer is on the floor at every hour, day or night, and it's included.
