About

A local gym
that stays open

Al Mannai Gym is a men's fitness centre in Al Alawi Mall, Nuwaidrat, in Sanad. We are open every hour of every day, because the people who train here work shifts, study, and have families — and a gym that closes at ten is no use to them.

We put the money into things you use: a proper strength floor, a cardio centre with its own space, and a recovery suite with sauna, steam room and hot and cold jacuzzi that costs you nothing extra. We didn't put it into a juice bar or a wall of screens.

Our trainers are on the floor, not in an office selling packages. Ask them anything — that's what they're there for.
