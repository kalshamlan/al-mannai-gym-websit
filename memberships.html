Straight answers

Asked at reception
every week

Anything else — WhatsApp reception. Someone is there at every hour.

Is there a joining fee?
No. The plan price is the whole price — no joining fee, no facility surcharge, no charge for the sauna, steam room or jacuzzi.
Is it really open 24 hours?
Yes — every hour of every day, including public holidays, with someone at reception at all times. Train at 3am or 3pm; it's the same membership.
Can I cancel?
The 1-month plan is rolling — you can simply not renew. Longer plans run to their end date; that's what makes them cheaper per month.
How does the loyalty rate work?
Join on any plan of three months or longer at the standard rate. Renew before it lapses and the loyalty rate applies — up to 27.5 BHD off a year — and it stays as long as you keep renewing on time.
Do I pay extra for the recovery suite?
No. Sauna, steam room and the hot & cold jacuzzi are included in every plan, at every hour the gym is open — which is all of them.
Who can join?
Al Mannai Gym is a men's fitness centre for ages 18 and over. Bring your CPR or passport on your first visit.
